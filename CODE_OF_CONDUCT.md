# Code of Conduct

Contributors must keep collaboration respectful, specific, and focused on the
work. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, and the
publication of private manuscript or review material are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns through a private repository contact channel when one
is available. Do not publish sensitive details in an issue. Maintainers may
edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when necessary.

This policy applies to repository discussions, reviews, issues, pull requests,
and other project spaces.
